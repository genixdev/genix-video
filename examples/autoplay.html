Play on arrival.

Autoplay explicitly allows initial loading. Muting helps, but browser policy can still block playback. If blocked, use the original player controls. Closing restores manual Play without restarting autoplay.

You can pause or close this silent sample. The same attributes work with provider=youtube and a YouTube ID.
