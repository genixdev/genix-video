Same video. A moving background.

Blur in motion.

The foreground stays sharp and complete. A second muted copy fills the background, enlarged and blurred. The poster remains underneath while it loads or if playback is blocked. Nothing starts until you press Play.

This optional effect uses more network and decoding resources. The two copies follow play, pause and close; exact frame synchronization is not required.

YouTube

Two independent players share the same API download. Ads, loading and browser playback policies can differ. The background has no interaction or keyboard focus.
